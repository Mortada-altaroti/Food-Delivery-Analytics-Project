# Food Delivery Analytics Project

## Project Overview

This project analyzes food delivery operations to uncover insights related to delivery performance, customer satisfaction, traffic conditions, weather impact, and operational efficiency.

The project combines Python, SQL, and Power BI to perform end-to-end data analytics and business intelligence analysis.

---

## Tools & Technologies

* Python (Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib)
* SQL (SQLite)
* Power BI
* Jupyter Notebook

---

## Project Workflow

1. Data Cleaning & Preprocessing
2. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
3. SQL Business Analysis
4. Power BI Dashboard Development
5. Business Insights & Visualization

---

## Key Business Questions

* What increases delivery delays?
* Which vehicle performs best?
* Which conditions reduce operational efficiency?
* How do traffic and weather affect customer satisfaction?
* What factors are associated with low customer satisfaction?

---

## Key Insights

* High traffic conditions significantly increased delivery times.
* Longer delivery distances were strongly associated with delivery delays.
* Bike deliveries achieved the highest customer satisfaction levels.
* Severe weather conditions reduced operational efficiency.
* Customer satisfaction decreased under difficult traffic and weather conditions.
